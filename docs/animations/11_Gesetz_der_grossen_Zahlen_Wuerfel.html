Blue: relative frequency (run 1 bold). Dashed red: 1/6. Green band: ±2 standard errors, √(p(1−p)/n), around 1/6. It narrows like 1/√n.

Absolute deviation from the expected count

Count minus n/6. Green band: ±2√(n·p·(1−p)). The band widens with n.

Take-away. The relative frequency converges to 1/6, but the absolute difference between the count and n/6 does not shrink; it typically grows like √n. The die has no memory and does not "make up" for earlier deviations. The law of large numbers works by dilution, not by compensation.
