Which random process generates each row?
RowProcess used for the simulation

Click again and again: almost every cell changes, but around the same centre. Nothing in a published table is “the” truth about a group. It is one realisation of a random process, and a new trial from the same populations would print slightly different numbers.

Look at the medians and quartiles of the two skewed rows and at the small percentages (“< 1”, “1”) in the confidence rows: rare categories and skewed durations fluctuate much more than the mean of a roughly symmetric scale.
