Quantitative Methods 1, ZHAW

eLearning 9.11. - 13.11.

1. Review the material

Go through everything we have covered together. Read the script at your own pace and revisit the animations we showed in class. You are welcome to extend the animations with further elements that you find useful (using coding tools).

2. Standard deviations that are too large

In class we noticed that Table 1 of a paper often reports a standard deviation that is too large for the scale of a (semi-)continuous variable. Create realistic examples with scales you know and reproduce this phenomenon.

  • What do the raw data look like in such cases?
  • What goes wrong?

3. Exercises

Please work on the following exercises of the chapter on probability:

  • Exercise 2 (Bayes-teaser)
  • Exercise 3 (Find journals and papers): In addition, please pick at least one paper that is particularly relevant for you right away. We will discuss some of them together in class and look at the methods critically. Publications with data and code would be ideal, although they are probably rare. We need a few days of lead time so that everybody can look at the papers beforehand. I would not be surprised if we find errors.
  • Exercise 4 (Independent and disjoint)
  • Exercise 6 (Three researchers)
  • Exercise 7 (Conditional probability): In addition, invent an example of your own with a single throw of a die.
